The History of Whitewater Rafting

Origins

White water rafting has a rich history that dates back to the mid-19th century. The sport originated in the United States, particularly in the western regions where rivers with challenging rapids were abundant.

Modern Era

The modern sport of white water rafting began to take shape in the 1950s and 1960s when inflatable rafts became more widely available. Today, it is a well-established adventure sport worldwide with thousands of outfitters serving adventure seekers.
